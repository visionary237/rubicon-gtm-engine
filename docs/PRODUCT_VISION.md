# Product vision

## Positioning
**Democratize GTM Engineering.** Turn commercial objectives into qualified, costed, personalised and executable acquisition plans for teams that can't afford a Clay + 6sense + Gong stack or a RevOps engineer.

RUBICON is not "more leads". It is the layer **between** data tools and salespeople: what to buy (and what not to), who should act, with which offer, when, and how to learn from it.

## Differentiators (hypotheses to validate)
1. **Internal profiles, not just customer ICPs**: an Ideal Sales Profile and an Ideal Offer Profile matched to each opportunity.
2. **Promises before leads**: follow-ups are protected by design, not by discipline.
3. **Cost-gated waterfall**: you pay only for qualified accounts, after an explicit quote.
4. **Explainable by default**: every decision has contributions, alternatives and an override.
5. **Evidence over notes**: post-call sync checks calendars and sent emails.
6. **Skills as assets**: business know-how becomes reusable, versioned Claude Skills.

## Targets
SMBs selling B2B, agencies, consulting firms, sales and growth teams, RevOps, founders doing their own sales. First design partners: **Proper Group** and **DOERZ** (the owner's companies, in private workspaces).

## Pilot offer (sellable this week, no SaaS required)
**"RUBICON Pilot — 14 days, one vertical, one offer"** — hypothesis 990 € excl. tax:
1. Day 1–2: `/outbound-setup` with the client (context, offer, vertical, dictionary, reps, CRM).
2. Day 3: first batch of 50 qualified accounts, reviewed together; enrichment quote approved by the client (credits at the client's cost).
3. Day 4–14: daily 8 a.m. roadmaps and call briefs, evening post-call sync, weekly learning report.
4. Deliverables: workspace, CRM, dictionary, script, the client's own batch file, a final report with real denominators.
**No promise of results.** The client owns data and credentials.
